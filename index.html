Level 1The whole lifecycle

Every vehicle is a loop.

Ore becomes steel, steel becomes parts, and parts become one of the record 2.83 crore vehicles shipped to Indian dealers in FY26. Then each one is financed, driven, serviced, resold, scrapped and melted back into steel. Pick any stop to see who does what.

Goods move clockwise in the outer lane.Money moves the other way in the inner lane, keeping left as Indian traffic does.

Level 3Inside a car

Same body, two different hearts.

An electric car deletes the engine, fuel system, exhaust and gearbox, keeps almost everything around them, and adds a battery, a motor and power electronics. Flip the plate to compare, then tap any part to see who makes it in India.

Level 4Inside a part

How the power is made.

A petrol, diesel or CNG engine turns small explosions into rotation through a piston, a rod and a crankshaft. An electric motor does it with a magnetic field that the inverter spins around the rotor. Tap any labelled part to see who makes it.
